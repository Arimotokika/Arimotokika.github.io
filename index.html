Academic Biography

About Me

I am a Lecturer in the Department of Software Engineering at Green University of Bangladesh (GUB) and an incoming part-time M.Sc. student in Computer Science and Engineering at Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET), where I completed my B.Sc. in CSE in 2026 (CGPA 3.74/4.00). Previously, I served as a Lecturer in the Department of AI & Data Science at Dhaka International University (DIU).

My research spans visual graph reasoning with Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs), low-resource Bangla speech recognition and speaker diarization, and evolutionary combinatorial optimization. Alongside research, I build open-source systems for satellite river monitoring, urban safety, hardware cryptanalysis, and network security.
